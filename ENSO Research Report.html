Market overview

We are mapping the French market for a Chief Product Officer at Enso, a critical hire to lead the cloud and AI rebuild of its core ERP, TEMPO, alongside the integration of Coffreo and clicNwork. This role follows two acquisitions and sits in a young product function, within PSG Equity’s value-creation window and an expected exit in 12 to 18 months.

The CPO will join the executive team and act as a key partner to the CEO, CTO and CRO, with ownership of:

  • Product strategy and suite architecture across TEMPO, Coffreo and clicNwork
  • Product-market fit for the new SaaS ERP and migration of the installed base
  • AI delivery and its commercialisation
  • Build-out of the product organisation, including UX/design and product marketing

Candidate Insights

We map candidates across CPO, CPTO and senior product leadership profiles to reflect the full market. So far, the majority of relevant profiles sit at C-suite level (63 of 88), with a further 20 VPs who could step up. The 3 Heads of Product are the most senior product person in their organisations, as titles vary.

The overall pool remains narrow when combining location, C-suite scope with a product background, with only 25 of the 63 C-suite profiles coming from product and most of the rest from engineering. 11 hold CPTO titles, which tend to be technology-led, so the VPs Product beneath them offer a useful step-up pool, though they probably lack board exposure. Diversity is also limited (~15% female), reflecting broader market constraints.

Candidate Location

The Paris region holds 60 of the 88 candidates mapped (68%), with a smaller cluster around Lyon (6) and the rest spread across regional hubs such as Nantes, Bordeaux and Lille. Only one candidate is based in Alsace, in the Strasbourg area, and none in Colmar.

Given the expectation of 2 to 3 days a week in Colmar, we foresee some location friction, meaning we will likely need to flex on some criteria to ensure a healthy pipeline. Approaches could include:

  • Step-up candidates
  • Broader domain focus
  • Flexibility on location

We will evaluate candidates against their executive influence, ability to articulate and execute a compelling product vision, track record of SaaS migration, product organisational leadership and commercialisation of AI-enabled capabilities. Exposure to PE sponsors, successful exits and post-merger integration will provide further differentiation. French fluency will be essential, while proximity to Colmar and willingness to establish a meaningful on-site presence will be assessed from the outset.

Initial Findings

Location: The majority of the pool sit outside of Colmar, with Paris-based profiles dominating as the main hub, so location terms will need to flex early to ensure a healthy pipeline.
C-suite vs VP: Few of the C-suite profiles come from a product background, and 11 hold CPTO-style titles that lean technical. The VPs Product beneath them are a closer fit for the scope and a natural step-up pool, although they probably have less board exposure.
Availability: Seven profiles are founders, fractional or recently moved, so the pool that is realistically open to a conversation is smaller than it looks.
Legacy-to-SaaS migration: This can’t be judged from LinkedIn profiles, so we will need to screen each candidate individually on it, alongside languages.
